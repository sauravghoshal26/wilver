# Wilver product brief

## Promise

Wilver helps adult pet parents form safe, useful local relationships without publishing home addresses, exact coordinates, private health records or direct contact details.

The north-star metric is meaningful, safe local connections per active member per month. Supporting measures are activation, seven-day retention, accepted activity requests, useful lost-pet sightings, block/report rate, moderation response time and crash-free sessions.

## Launch scope

- Email authentication, confirmation, recovery, secure sessions and account deletion
- Versioned adult/legal consent and optional foreground-location onboarding
- Real parent profiles and multiple pet profiles with owner-reported versus moderator-verified health signals
- Posts, sanitized photos, likes, comments, deletion and reports
- GPS-backed, block-aware approximate profile discovery and mutual connection requests
- Walks, playdates and events with join approval, capacity and protected meeting instructions
- Realtime one-to-one chat with image attachments, nonce deduplication, database rate limits and blocking
- Lost/found alerts, urgent nearby notifications, private sightings and in-app owner contact
- In-app and opt-in push notifications with allowlisted deep links
- Privacy controls, moderation removal, audit records and suspended-account enforcement
- iOS/Android release profiles and no-credential fail-closed behavior

The launch client supports dogs and cats and assumes English-first operation. Market, legal entity, final launch geography and regional data obligations are founder decisions—not hardcoded product claims.

## Explicit non-goals

- No animal marketplace or sales
- No emergency, veterinary, legal or identity-verification service
- No public exact location
- No automated claim that an owner-reported vaccine status is verified
- No responsible-breeding launch until independent legal, veterinary-welfare and moderation approval
- No monetization until trust and retention are demonstrated

## Primary journeys

1. A confirmed adult creates an account, reviews policies, optionally shares GPS, creates a pet and joins an initially empty real community.
2. A member finds approximate nearby profiles, sends a mutual request and starts an eligible direct conversation.
3. A host creates an activity with a general area and private meeting instructions, then approves attendees.
4. A member creates a lost alert and receives privacy-safe sightings and chat without publishing contact details.
5. A moderator reviews a report; removal and an audit record occur in one server transaction.

## Founder and operations decisions still required

- Launch market, operating entity and governing law
- Counsel-approved Terms, Privacy Notice, retention schedule and community standards
- Confirmed support mailbox/domain and data-rights workflow
- Moderation staffing, escalation hours, appeals and law-enforcement request handling
- Verification partners and what qualifies for identity, vaccination or health verification
- Whether responsible breeding should ever exist, with independent welfare experts holding veto authority
- Analytics, crash monitoring, incident paging and privacy-safe KPI definitions
